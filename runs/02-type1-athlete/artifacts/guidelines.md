## Applicable guidance

Public ADA/CDC pages support matching carbohydrate to activity. This run uses 350–400 g/day, with extra portions near training — not insulin-dose advice.

### Eating pattern
Three meals plus training snacks. Include a measured fast-acting carbohydrate option for lows.

### Sodium / cardiovascular
No personal cap. Include fluids with longer sessions.

### Hypoglycemia (include only if relevant)

### What this means for this run
