Weekly diet guide

Type 1 endurance pattern · sample run 02

This weekly plan is an educational artifact from an agentic workflow. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or a substitute for care from a clinician or registered dietitian. Insulin and carbohydrate ratios must only be changed with a qualified professional.

Profile: Age 28, insulin pump, five endurance sessions per week, lows around long runs.

Targets: about 3000 kcal · 350–400 g carb · no grocery cap.

How to use this week

Batch rice and pasta. Eat the banana snack 30–60 minutes before afternoon runs. Carry 15 g glucose tablets. Do not change pump settings from this document.

Grocery list

Oats, rice, pasta, sourdough, peanut butter, juice, glucose tablets, bananas, apples, berries, broccoli, sweet potatoes, chicken, salmon, yogurt, eggs, olive oil.

Recipes

Training breakfast bowl; pasta and chicken; salmon rice plate; pre-run banana with peanut butter.

Safety

Rescue carbohydrate: 15 g glucose tablets (orange juice if tablets are missing), consistent with public CDC hypoglycemia pages. Seek urgent care for severe lows. This guide does not set insulin doses.
